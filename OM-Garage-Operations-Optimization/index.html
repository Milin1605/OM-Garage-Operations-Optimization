Example: 3 cars + 1 worker → 1 active Dry Bay slot, 1 worker/slot; 3 cars + 4 workers → 2 active slots, 2 workers/slot. This avoids a zero-worker division error. It remains a simplified staffing model, not a validated shift-level schedule.

Cycle Time (CT) — bottleneck rate
CT = max(Wet Bay CT, Dry Bay CT, Extra Bay CT)
Dry Bay CT = (station time per car) ÷ slots.
Workers/slot = floor(totalW ÷ active slots). Max benefit = 2W/slot.
Why the old formula was wrong
Old: wpc = totalW ≥ cars×2 ? 2 : 1
For 4W, 3 cars: 4 ≥ 6 → wpc=1 (wrong!)
New: wpc = min(2, floor(4 ÷ 2 slots)) = 2 ✓
Dry Bay has 2 slots → 4W ÷ 2 slots = 2W each.
