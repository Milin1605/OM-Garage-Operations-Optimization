# Assumptions and Limitations

1. Wet Bay process time is fixed at 110 minutes.
2. Dry Bay has two physical slots; Extra Bay has one.
3. The simulator uses a simplified worker-per-slot rule, with at most two workers per active Dry Bay slot.
4. Variable task times are reduced using the worker allocation rule; this assumes productivity scales in the represented way.
5. Queue wait is approximated as (cars − 1) × 110 minutes. It is not derived from a simulated queue.
6. A day contains two 12-hour shifts, with no breaks, handovers, downtime or demand variation.
7. Permanent labour is costed at ₹66.66 per hour and temporary labour at ₹322 per hour.
8. The 15% revenue allowance is a placeholder assumption, not a verified cost ratio.
9. Rent, utilities, materials, taxes, maintenance, marketing, depreciation and other overheads are not comprehensively included.
10. The same simplified workforce-per-slot value is applied across variable activities; actual staffing may require dedicated teams and skill constraints.

Use real operational observations to validate every input before relying on the outputs for business decisions.
