# How to Interpret the Simulator

## Bottleneck
The station with the highest modelled cycle time is identified as the bottleneck. Improving a non-bottleneck station may not increase overall throughput if another station remains slower.

## Capacity and staffing
Adding workers can reduce the modelled duration of variable tasks, but the benefit is limited by the number of physical slots and the two-worker cap. Once Wet Bay becomes the bottleneck, additional labour may not reduce system cycle time under the current assumptions.

## Throughput
The application estimates shift throughput from the cycle-time value. This is a theoretical capacity estimate; actual output can be lower because of demand, breaks, rework, downtime, variability and other constraints not represented.

## Financial interpretation
The displayed “indicative surplus” subtracts a 15% revenue allowance and estimated labour cost per car from package revenue. It is not net profit, EBITDA or a verified contribution margin because several cost categories are excluded and the allowance is assumed.

## Managerial use
Use the simulator to frame questions for a real process study: measure task times, observe queues, identify resource conflicts, validate staffing and build a cost model from actual records.
